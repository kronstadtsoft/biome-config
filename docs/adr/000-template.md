# ADR-NNN — Short title in the form of the decision

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted (D Mon YYYY) | Superseded by ADR-NNN | Deprecated
**Context owners:**

## Context

What is true that forces a decision. Constraints, the state of the world, what was measured or
researched and when. Enough that someone arriving in a year understands the pressure without asking.

Write this in the present tense of the decision, not with hindsight.

## Decision

The decision, stated flatly. One paragraph or a short list — not a discussion.

## Why

The reasoning that made this the answer rather than the alternatives. Name the alternatives that
were seriously considered and why they lost; an ADR with no rejected option is usually a decision
that was never really made.

## Consequences

What this commits us to, including the parts that are inconvenient. What becomes easy, what becomes
hard, what has to be revisited and under what trigger.

If the decision has a legal, accessibility or data-protection consequence, say so here explicitly
and link the relevant entry in the QMS legal register.
